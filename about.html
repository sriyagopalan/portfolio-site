About

Designing for curious, young and different minds.

I’m Sriya, a senior at San José State University earning a BA in Design Studies with a concentration in Graphic Design. I’ve been making art since I was five; as a visual learner, drawing has always been how I understand things.

I studied piano for twelve years and won several competitions in the Bay Area. Today I volunteer with kids with special needs through music and art, and I want to illustrate children’s books that help kids feel calm, capable and included.
